
Guided mode runs all seven steps:
understand, plan, ask one question at a time, wait for your approval, create with explanations, verify, deliver. Direct mode skips the questions and the approval, works from what you gave it, and labels its assumptions. The template allows Direct only when you select it and set plan approval to Not required.

When to use which:
Guided for anything new or unclear, such as a test plan from a PRD, a framework, or requirements with gaps. Direct for small, well-defined repeats, such as adding five negative cases to a plan you already approved, or turning test cases into a table.
